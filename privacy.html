Privacy & cookies

Updated 15 October 2026

Veena Studios uses optional Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser information and interactions such as outbound link clicks or downloads. Google processes this information for analytics. We do not use this setup for advertising personalization or Google Signals.

The Google Analytics script loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”. You may reject analytics and continue using the website. Change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” below. Rejecting after previously allowing stops future collection after the page reloads; it does not erase data already collected or existing cookies. You can remove cookies through your browser settings.

We store your cookie preference and, if selected, an owner-visit exclusion flag in your browser's local storage. Your browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal also prevents analytics loading. Blocking storage or clearing browser data may reset your choices.

Analytics counts are estimates. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or phone numbers to Google Analytics. Avoid entering personal information into website URLs.

Read how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google's privacy policy.
