# Tap Rush — Privacy Policy (publication draft)
**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

Tap Rush is a mobile game developed under the SR GAMERS / Veena Studios brand. This draft must be reviewed against the final Android build and published at a public URL before Play Store submission.

## Information stored on your device
The game stores local progress, coin balance, world unlocks, high scores, and sound preferences using on-device storage. This information supports gameplay and can be lost if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. We have not implemented a player account or cloud backup.

## Advertising
The current development/debug version uses Google AdMob test rewarded advertisements. Ads are optional and are shown only after the player requests them. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, diagnostics and ad interaction data when initialized. The current release build gates off rewarded ad initialization and the ad bridge; this statement must be rechecked if production ads are enabled.

If production ads are introduced, this policy will be updated with accurate data practices, any required consent choices, and applicable Google advertising disclosures before publishing.

## Purchases
Paid unlock packs are planned but are not currently enabled. If purchases are introduced, Google Play Billing will handle payment transactions; we do not intend to collect payment-card details directly. This policy must be updated before billing is launched.

## Network access
Core gameplay assets are bundled for offline play. External Veena Studios links and any future optional advertisements require an internet connection.

## Children's privacy
The intended target audience and any age-related protections must be finalized in Play Console before publication. Do not select a child-directed audience without first checking SDK and Families requirements.
