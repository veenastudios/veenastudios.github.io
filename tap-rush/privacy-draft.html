Tap Rush — Privacy Information

Veena Studios / SR GAMERS · Draft for review · Updated 12 November 2026

Not yet the final Play Store privacy policy. This document describes the current development configuration. It must be reviewed against the exact production app, advertising setup and contact details before publication.

Game data stored on your device

Tap Rush stores scores, game preferences, coin balances and unlocked worlds locally on your device to keep your game progress. Removing the app may remove this locally stored progress. The core game does not require an account.

Advertising

Development builds use Google's test rewarded ads. Players can choose to watch a rewarded video for in-game coins. Test ads do not represent a live production advertising setup. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process identifiers and diagnostic information when used. Any production ad configuration and consent mechanism must be assessed and disclosed before release. See Google Privacy Policy.

Payments

Paid world-unlock products are under development and are not currently available for verified purchases. If introduced, payments will be handled through Google Play Billing and the final policy will be updated to describe the relevant purchase verification and processing.

Sharing and retention

Local gameplay progress is retained on the device until it is cleared or the application is removed. The final production version may involve external providers for advertising and purchase verification; their actual data practices must be reviewed before this draft is finalized.
