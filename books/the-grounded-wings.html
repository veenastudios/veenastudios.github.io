THE STORY BEHIND THE BOOK

THIS STORY WASN’T IMAGINED.
IT WAS LIVED.

The Grounded Wings is the English-language edition of the same autobiographical journey first published in Hindi as आकाश की ज़मीनी उड़ान. It follows an ordinary boy from an ordinary family. He had dreams, plans and a future in his mind—but very little idea of what life was preparing for him.

He thought he was moving toward his future.
Life was quietly preparing its own turns.

There were battles he had never imagined, losses he could not plan for, and moments that forced him to gather himself and begin again.

Years later, that same journey would become a book—and the beginning of something he had never originally set out to build: Veena Studios, a growing world of books, games, videos and ideas.

But this is not a story about what exists today.
The real question is: how did an ordinary boy reach here?

What did he see? What did he lose? What did he survive? And after all of it, why did he keep moving?

The answers live inside these pages.

And perhaps the story still isn’t finished…
because the hunger to build is still alive.

A few questions. The answers are inside the book.

Edition note: This is the English translation of आकाश की ज़मीनी उड़ान, not a separate life story. This preview presents moments from that same autobiography in English.

This preview does not reveal the story. It takes you only to the moments where the questions begin.

PREVIEW • 01

There was a boy. He had dreams. He had plans for the future.

He believed life might follow something close to the path he had imagined.

Then came 2007. What happened that began changing the way he saw life itself?

And in 2009, life took him somewhere a young man rarely imagines himself—through a kidney transplant.

But perhaps the operation was not the only question…
What was waiting for him on the day he returned home?

PREVIEW • 02

And then, love entered his life.

A love that, for a while, seemed larger than the battles that had come before it. Two people came so close that the natural ending of their story should have been together.

Then why did they separate?

Was love not enough? Or can two people lose a relationship even when the love itself has not disappeared?

If they loved each other, what made staying together impossible?

He had survived battles that once seemed impossible. So why was love the battle he could not win?

The answer is inside the book.
